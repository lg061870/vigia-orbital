For the technical questions: these are the coefficients of the five models, one per day ahead, trained on 2012 to 2025 with standardized inputs. History stays close to one: the model starts from the normal year. Weather has its biggest effect on tomorrow and fades with distance, while vegetation becomes more important the further ahead we look — it’s a slow signal. Terrain is essentially zero at canton level. The estimation is maximum likelihood; our own Newton–Raphson converged in five iterations and matched the standard statsmodels result exactly.
