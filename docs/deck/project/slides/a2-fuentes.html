Repository: github.com/lg061870/vigia-orbital (MIT)

All data is open. NASA FIRMS for fire detections from four satellites; NASA POWER for daily weather; NASA MODIS products for the vegetation indices, accessed through Microsoft Planetary Computer; NASADEM for terrain; NASA GIBS and Worldview for the satellite images; NOAA for the El Niño index; NASA ARSET for the manual workflow reference. Plus ESA WorldCover, SINAC’s SIREFOR, geoBoundaries, OpenStreetMap through the Humanitarian Data Exchange, Kontur and UNFPA population, and Leaflet. Full citations and licences are in the repository README.
