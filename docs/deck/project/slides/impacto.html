Who benefits? Guanacaste concentrates 63 percent of Costa Rica’s fire detections, and 88 percent of them happen in just three months. With a five-day forecast, SINAC’s fire program and the volunteer brigades can decide where to position crews and equipment, canton by canton. Firefighters and the national emergency commission can decide when to scale up — and when to stand down: at the end of the season the model sees the rains coming, 23 percent better than the calendar. And municipalities can time prevention messages and burn controls to the actual risk. In the field, the forecast reaches brigades as a plain text message under one kilobyte, so it works even with a weak phone signal. The message shown uses the real forecast for the demo week (January 2018).
