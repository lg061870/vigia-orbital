The Risk of Fire Index

A machine learning model built on NASA data · 5 days ahead · the 11 cantons of Guanacaste, Costa Rica

What it is

  • For each canton and each of the next five days: the probability that satellites detect a vegetation fire.
  • One number from 0 to 1, with a level:

Low

Moderate

High

Very high

Extreme

  • Every value comes with its reasons: weather, vegetation, recent fire.

Built on NASA data

  • NASA FIRMS: MODIS and VIIRS hot spots from four satellites, harmonized into one fire record (2012–2026).
  • NASA POWER: daily weather.
  • NASA MODIS: vegetation greenness and moisture (NDVI, NDMI).
  • NASADEM: terrain.

Model: one logistic regression per day ahead

Why it matters

  • Guanacaste holds 63 % of Costa Rica’s fire detections, 88 % of them in February–April.
  • Five days of warning lets brigades be in the right place before the fire.
