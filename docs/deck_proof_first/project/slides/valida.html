2026 test season: a tie, said honestly

Does it work? One blind week could be luck, so I tested it the strict way: for each season from 2013 to 2025, I trained the model on all the other years and forecast that season blind. Overall, its probabilities are 8.4 percent more accurate than simply using an average year, and it wins in 12 of 14 seasons — most in the worst ones: 2013 by 21 percent, 2022 by 17, 2024 by 15. It’s also reliable at the extremes: when it said 90 percent or more, fire happened 96 percent of the time, over 7,900 canton-days. And it holds up over the five days: plus 7.8 percent tomorrow, still plus 5.4 percent on day five. To be honest: on the 2026 season, a true test it never saw, it tied with the average year — a season with nothing unusual for the conditions to explain. Metric: Brier skill score versus the canton-and-week climatology; AUC 0.932 vs 0.919.
