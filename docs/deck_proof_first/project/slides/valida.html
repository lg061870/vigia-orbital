2026 test season: a tie, said honestly

But one good week could be luck. So I repeated the test… for every fire season since 2013. Each time, I hid one whole season from the model. It learned from all the others… and then had to forecast the season it had never seen. I compared it with the simplest forecast there is: assume this year will be like an average year. The model came out ahead… in twelve seasons out of fourteen. In the WORST fire seasons, it was up to twenty-one percent more accurate. And when it said ninety percent or more… fire came, ninety-six times in a hundred. One honest note: in 2026, the margin was almost nothing. A tie.
