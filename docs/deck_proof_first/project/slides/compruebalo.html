Live, open and reproducible. Pick any date since 2012 and compare the forecast with what happened

1 · The live forecast

lg061870.github.io/vigia-orbital

  • Every canton of Guanacaste, five days ahead.
  • Turn back the clock: any date since 2012.
  • For past dates it shows the forecast and what the satellites detected afterwards.
  • Each day explains why: weather, vegetation, recent fire.

2 · The open API

…hf.space/api/docs

  • Computes each forecast on request; nothing is stored.
  • Any system can call it: one web address per canton and date.
  • The same forecast as a text message under 1 KB, for brigades with weak signal.

Science asks us not to believe… but to check. So everything here is open. A website, where you can choose any canton, any date since 2012, and see what the forecast said… and what truly happened. An open service that answers on request… even as a simple text message, small enough for a single bar of signal. Website: https://lg061870.github.io/vigia-orbital/ · API: https://lg061870-vigia-orbital-api.hf.space/api/docs · Note: forecasts for past dates on the site use the official model, trained on those years; the strict blind tests are the demo week and the season-by-season validation.
