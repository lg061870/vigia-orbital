Harmonizing MODIS + VIIRS

Four satellites, one consistent fire record (Guanacaste, 2012–2026)

Vegetation-fire detections per year, by satellite

Source: NASA FIRMS, standard processing (near-real-time after Jul/Aug 2026), vegetation fires only

The problem

  • VIIRS (375 m) sees 3.5–5.4× more fires than MODIS (1 km), and the ratio changes by year and canton: no single conversion factor works.
  • From 2018 a second VIIRS satellite, from 2023 a third: more satellites, not more fire.

Our harmonized measure

  • One reference satellite per period: S-NPP (2012–2025), then NOAA-20.
  • Count fire days per canton (yes/no), not raw detections.
  • Checked: NOAA-21 ÷ S-NPP = 1.01, 0.96, 0.97 (2023–25). The record continues after S-NPP ended (Nov 2026).
Before any forecast, there was a harder problem. Four satellites have watched these fires… and each sees the world differently. One sees up to FIVE times more fires than another. Add a satellite, and the numbers leap… though the fires have not changed. So I chose one witness at a time. And I counted not the sparks… but the DAYS with fire. The newest satellite agrees with the old one within four percent. The record… is unbroken.
