Tuesday the 23rd: High risk, 0.39, and the satellites did detect fire in Liberia that day. Where does the number come from? History first: in a normal year, Liberia has a 45% chance of fire on this date, and fire was detected on 23 January in 7 of the other 13 years. Then this year’s conditions adjust it: on the eve it had rained and the air was humid, which lowers the odds (×0.65); the vegetation was slightly drier than normal (×1.18) and there had been fire nearby in the last week (×1.25). Result: 39%, High. The terrain indicators come from NASA’s MODIS satellites: greenness (NDVI), leaf moisture (NDMI) and the burn index (NBR), all close to normal for mid-January, with no recent burn scar. Note: the multipliers act on the odds, not directly on the percentage. Values from the notebook (C29), model trained without any 2018 data.
