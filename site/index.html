About Vigía Orbital

Does it work?

Tested season by season, 2013–2025: each season is forecast by a model that never saw it. Skill is measured against an average year (the normal for that canton and week).

Risk levels

How it works

  1. Harmonize NASA FIRMS hot spots from MODIS and VIIRS (S-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21): one reference satellite per period, vegetation fires only, fire days per canton.
  2. Combine with NASA POWER daily weather and MODIS vegetation indices (NDVI, NDMI).
  3. Forecast with one logistic model per day ahead: the normal risk for the canton and week, adjusted by weather, vegetation and recent fire.

Sources

NASA FIRMS (MODIS and VIIRS hot spots) · NASA POWER (weather) · NASA MODIS MOD13Q1/MYD13Q1 and MOD09A1/MYD09A1 via Microsoft Planetary Computer · NASA GIBS (satellite images) · geoBoundaries (cantons).
