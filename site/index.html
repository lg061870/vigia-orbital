Fire-risk forecast · Guanacaste, Costa Rica

Liberia

Why this forecast?

The model starts from the normal risk for this canton and week of the year, then adjusts it with the current conditions.

Latest observed conditions

How good is it?

Validated leave-one-season-out over 2013–2025: each season is forecast by a model that never saw it. Skill is measured against the climatology (the normal for that canton and week).

How it works

  1. Harmonize NASA FIRMS hot spots from MODIS and VIIRS (S-NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) into one consistent record: one reference satellite per period, vegetation fires only, a fire-day flag per canton and local date.
  2. Combine with NASA POWER daily weather and MODIS vegetation indices (NDVI, NDMI).
  3. Forecast with one logistic model per lead day: the normal risk for the canton and week, adjusted by weather, vegetation dryness and recent fire.
